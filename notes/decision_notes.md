|6|26/11/26|Question 1: choice of model for goals scored|The goals-scored response is a count, so Poisson regression with a log link was used. Four candidate models were compared: an intercept-only model, a model with opponent rank, a model with opponent rank and venue, and a model including an interaction between opponent rank and venue. AIC and likelihood-ratio tests were used to assess whether additional terms improved the model.| The additive model containing opponent rank and home/away status was selected. It had the lowest AIC (133.43). Adding venue significantly improved the model after accounting for opponent rank (LRT p = 0.009), whereas the interaction did not provide a significant improvement (LRT p = 0.548).| Selected for question 1|
|7|26/11/26|Question 1: interpretation of opponent rank and venue|Opponent rank is defined such that rank 1 represents the strongest team and rank 24 the weakest. The estimated opponent-rank rate ratio was 1.056, indicating approximately 5.6% higher expected goals for each one-place increase in the opponent's rank, holding venue constant. The estimated home-versus-away rate ratio was 2.00, indicating twice the expected goals at home compared with away, holding opponent rank constant.|Retain both explanatory variables in the selected model. Interpret the relationships as associations rather than causal effects.|Included in Question 1 findings
|8|26/11/26|Question 1: assessment of model fit and dispersion|The selected Poisson model was assessed using residual deviance, a goodness-of-fit test and Pearson dispersion. The residual deviance was 49.78 on 43 degrees of freedom (approximate p = 0.221), while the Pearson dispersion estimate was approximately 0.99. These results did not indicate substantial overdispersion or clear evidence of poor overall fit. A quasi-Poisson comparison also produced very similar standard errors.|Retain the Poisson model as the primary model; report the diagnostic checks and their limitations.|Completed
|9|26/11/26|Question 1: influential observation and sensitivity analysis|Diagnostic checks identified Match 8, in which Ashford scored eight goals, as an influential observation. The match had a relatively large Cook's distance and standardised residual. The primary analysis retained all 46 matches because the observation is part of the dataset and should not be removed solely because it is unusual. A sensitivity analysis excluding Match 8 was also carried out to assess its influence on the conclusions.|Retain the full 46-match dataset for the primary analysis and report the 45-match sensitivity analysis separately. Excluding Match 8 weakened the statistical evidence for both explanatory variables, so this limitation should be acknowledged.|Completed
|10|26/11/26|Question 1: check of the functional form of opponent rank|A quadratic term for centred opponent rank was considered to investigate whether the relationship between opponent rank and expected goals might be nonlinear. The quadratic model had a slightly lower AIC (131.98 compared with 133.43), but the likelihood-ratio test did not provide sufficient evidence of improvement at the 5% significance level (p = 0.063).|Retain the simpler linear opponent-rank specification. Note that the quadratic check suggested possible curvature but did not provide sufficient evidence to justify the additional term.|Completed
